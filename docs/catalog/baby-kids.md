# Baby & Kids Vertical Overview

## Introduction
The Baby & Kids vertical in Gramer Bazar provides a safe, curated, and comprehensive destination for parents and families across Bangladesh. From newborn essentials and high-absorption diapers to pediatric-approved nutrition, educational toys, nursery furniture, and school supplies, every product is represented through the universal catalog engine.

## Key Features
- **Age-Driven & Weight-Driven Selection:** Direct filtering by age brackets (Newborn through 12+ Years) and baby weight ranges.
- **Diaper Sizing & Pack Dimensions:** Dedicated sizing axis (NB, S, M, L, XL, XXL) paired with unit count packs (20 pcs, 40 pcs, 60 pcs).
- **Safety, Hygiene & Caution Transparency:** Clear presentation of certifications (BPA-Free, Hypoallergenic, Tear-Free, Pediatrician Approved) and explicit warning labels (Adult Supervision, Choking Hazard).
- **Batch & Expiry Vigilance:** Strict expiry date and lot tracking for infant cereals, baby food, and antiseptic wipes.
- **Bilingual Storefront:** Full localization in English and Bengali with culturally authentic child care phrasing.
