# Home & Kitchen Vertical Overview

## Introduction
The **Home & Kitchen** (`home-kitchen`) catalog vertical brings kitchen appliances, cookware, dining essentials, furniture, bedding, lighting, and home improvement products onto the Gramer Bazar universal multi-vendor engine.

Built 100% on the universal catalog engine:
- **No separate tables** — Reuses universal `categories`, `product_types`, `attributes`, `attribute_options`, `products`, `product_variants`, and `inventory`.
- **Multi-Dimensional Variant Support** — Supports Capacity (`home-capacity`), Pack Size (`home-pack-size`), Bed Size (`home-bed-size`), and Dimensions (`home-dimensions`) combined with Color (`home-color`).
- **Warranty & Technical Guidance** — Built-in official manufacturer warranty tags, wattage indicators, and doorstep inspection guidelines.
- **Bilingual Experience** — Complete English and Bangla localization for categories, attributes, specifications, and customer advisory messaging.

## Seed Data Summary
- **Attributes**: 14 structured attributes
- **Categories**: 10 primary branches with 25+ granular categories
- **Brands**: 20 authentic regional and international brands (Walton, Vision, RFL, Gazi, Regal, Kiam, Hatil, Otobi, Philips, Panasonic, Prestige, Tefal, Singer, etc.)
- **Demo Products**: 10 realistic products featuring smart rice cookers, fast-boil kettles, granite frying pans, heavy-duty mixer grinders, airtight food containers, cotton king bedsheets, solid wood dining chairs, pedestal standing fans, spin mop bucket sets, and smart eye-comfort LED bulbs.
