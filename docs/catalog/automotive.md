# Automotive Vertical Overview

## Introduction
The Automotive vertical in Gramer Bazar provides a reliable, verified, and vehicle-compatible marketplace for car and motorcycle owners, drivers, and fleet managers in Bangladesh. From critical engine parts, brake friction components, and batteries to synthetic lubricants, high-traction tires, and smart electronic dash cams, every product adheres to strict vehicle compatibility standards.

## Key Capabilities
- **Vehicle Compatibility / Fitment Matching:** Structured matching by vehicle make, model, generation, and year range.
- **OEM vs. Aftermarket Transparency:** Clear classification of parts as OEM Genuine Factory Parts, OEM Equivalent, or Premium Aftermarket.
- **Variant-Aware Dimensional Axes:** Dedicated selectors for fluid viscosity (0W-20 through 20W-50), fluid capacity (1L, 4L, etc.), tire specifications (195/65 R15, 205/55 R16, etc.), and battery amperages (35 Ah through 75 Ah).
- **Safety & Professional Installation Guidance:** Dedicated advisories highlighting certified mechanic installation for brake systems and electrical wiring.
- **Bilingual Bengali Support:** Comprehensive localized terminology for automotive parts and specs.
