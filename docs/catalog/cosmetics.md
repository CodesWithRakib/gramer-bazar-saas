# Cosmetics & Personal Care Vertical Overview

## Introduction
The **Cosmetics & Personal Care** (`cosmetics`) vertical expands the universal multi-vendor catalog on Gramer Bazar to beauty, skincare, haircare, fragrances, and personal hygiene.

Built 100% on the universal catalog engine:
- **No separate tables** — Uses existing `categories`, `product_types`, `attributes`, `attribute_options`, `products`, `product_variants`, and `inventory`.
- **Dual-Axis Variant Selection** — Supports both **Shade** (`cosmetics-shade`) with visual hex color swatches and **Volume** (`cosmetics-volume`) with volume pill selectors.
- **Safety & Care Guidance** — Built-in dermatological patch test guidance banner and beauty badge indicators.
- **Bilingual Experience** — Complete English and Bangla localization for all categories, attributes, shades, and advisory messaging.

## Seed Data Summary
- **Attributes**: 14 structured attributes
- **Categories**: 8 primary subtrees with 35+ granular categories
- **Brands**: 21 regional and global brands (Meril, Kool, CeraVe, Maybelline, L'Oréal, The Body Shop, COSRX, Nivea, Vaseline, etc.)
- **Demo Products**: 10 realistic products featuring serums, matte foundations with shade swatches, lipsticks, sunscreens, cleansers, shampoos, perfumes, lotions, beard oils, and baby care.
