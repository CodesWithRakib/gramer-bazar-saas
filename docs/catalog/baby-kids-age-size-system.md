# Baby & Kids Age & Size System

## 1. Age-Based Sizing Structure
Baby apparel, educational toys, and gear utilize structured age ranges rather than ambiguous generic sizing:
- **Newborn (0–1 Month):** Specially proportioned for infants up to 4.5 kg. Features nickel-free front snaps for rapid umbilical-safe changes.
- **0–3 Months:** Rapid growth phase clothing with fold-over mitten cuffs.
- **3–6 Months:** Early teething and rolling stage apparel.
- **6–12 Months:** Crawling and exploration garments with reinforced knee sections.
- **1–2 Years & 2–3 Years:** Active toddler wear with elastic waistbands.
- **3–5 Years (Preschool) & 5–8 Years (Early School):** Durable cotton fabrics, school accessories, and structured shoes.
- **8–12 Years & 12+ Years:** Pre-teen and youth casual wear.

## 3. First Walkers Footwear Sizing
Infant footwear conforms to European standard metric inner lengths:
- **EU 18 / 19:** 10.5 – 11.5 cm foot length (Pre-walker soft booties)
- **EU 20 / 21:** 12.0 – 12.8 cm foot length (First walking sneakers with flexible honeycomb anti-slip soles)
- **EU 22 / 24:** 13.5 – 14.8 cm foot length (Active toddler outdoor play)
- **EU 26 / 28 / 30:** 16.0 – 19.0 cm foot length (Preschool and early school sneakers)

## 4. Safety & Advisory Protocol
1. **Choking Hazard Prevention:** Every toy containing parts under 3 cm requires the `Choking Hazard (Small Parts)` and `Adult Supervision Required` warnings.
2. **Food Contact Material Standard:** All bottles, cups, plates, and cutlery must declare `BPA Free`, `Phthalate Free`, and food-grade safety certifications.
3. **Cosmetic Dermatological Standards:** Baby washes, shampoos, and lotions require pH-balance, hypoallergenic testing, and tear-free formula confirmation.
