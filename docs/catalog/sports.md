# Sports & Fitness Vertical Overview

## Introduction
The Sports & Fitness vertical in Gramer Bazar provides a verified, athlete-focused marketplace for players, sports academies, fitness enthusiasts, and outdoor adventurers across Bangladesh. From international match-grade cricket bats and FIFA-quality footballs to precision carbon badminton rackets, heavy-duty home gym dumbells, motorized treadmills, and high-performance bicycles, all gear meets rigorous quality, ergonomics, and safety standards.

## Key Capabilities
- **Universal Architecture Integration:** Runs entirely on the generic catalog core with zero duplicated database tables (`Product`, `ProductVariant`, `SKU`, `Inventory`, `Category`, `Attribute`).
- **Comprehensive Sports Taxonomy:** 14 structured branches covering team sports (Cricket, Football, Basketball, Volleyball), racket sports (Badminton, Tennis, Table Tennis), gym & cardio (Dumbbells, Barbells, Treadmills), mind & body (Yoga & Pilates), active outdoor (Cycling, Swimming, Boxing), and athletic apparel/footwear.
- **Multi-Dimensional Equipment Variants:** Native variant switching on dumbbell weights (5kg, 10kg, 15kg), boxing glove weights (10oz, 12oz, 14oz), ball sizes (Size 4, Size 5), footwear EU sizes (41-45), and bicycle wheel diameters (26-inch, 27.5-inch).
- **Safety & Equipment Ergonomics Advisory:** Built-in guidance on certified protective gear, motor capacity limits, and equipment maintenance.
- **Bilingual Storefront:** Full English and Bangla localization for categories, product types, and technical attributes.
