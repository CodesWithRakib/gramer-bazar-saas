# Books & Stationery Vertical Overview

## Introduction
The Books & Stationery vertical in Gramer Bazar provides a trusted, curated catalog for readers, school/college students, university scholars, competitive examinees, and creative professionals across Bangladesh. Every listed book is guaranteed to be a 100% genuine original print from authorized publishers, accompanied by structured ISBN identification, edition history, author attributions, and paper/stationery specifications.

## Key Capabilities
- **Universal Architecture Integration:** Operates directly on the shared generic catalog core with zero duplicated database tables (`Product`, `ProductVariant`, `SKU`, `Inventory`, `Category`, `Attribute`).
- **Structured Book Metadata:** Native tracking of ISBN-10/13, Author, Publisher, Edition, Language, Page Count, and Binding Format.
- **Stationery & Paper Specifications:** Multi-dimensional variant tracking on Paper Size (A4, A5), Paper GSM (65–300 GSM), ruling types, pen ink colors, and bulk ream packs.
- **Original Print Guarantee:** Storefront advisory assuring customers of authentic publisher editions and clear return policies for printing or binding defects.
- **Bilingual Storefront:** Full English and Bengali localization for categories, authors, genres, and paper specifications.
