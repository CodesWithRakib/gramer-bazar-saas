# Toys, Games & Hobbies Vertical

The **Toys, Games & Hobbies** vertical (`toys-games-hobbies` / খেলনা, গেমস ও শখের জিনিস) on Gramer Bazar serves children, families, students, and hobbyists with a safe, curated, and diverse selection of recreational and developmental products.

## Core Features
1. **Child Safety by Design**: Strict enforcement of small parts warnings, age recommendations, and toxic chemical prohibitions (BPA-free, lead-safe, certified organic dyes).
2. **Cognitive & Developmental Play**: Structured facets for STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics), Montessori sensory tools, and logical problem solving.
3. **Multi-Player Entertainment**: Comprehensive board games and family card games with player counts, duration, and bilingual localized editions.
4. **Dynamic Technical Attributes**: Advanced parameters for RC vehicles, electric ride-on cars, 3D mechanical wood puzzles, and scale model kits.
5. **Universal Catalog Integration**: Complete interoperability with seller inventory, order processing, dynamic cart validation, reviews, wishlists, and bilingual search.
