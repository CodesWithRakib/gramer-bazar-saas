# Gramer Bazar System User Guide

## Introduction
Gramer Bazar is a multi-role e-commerce platform designed for rural and local markets. This guide covers the basic operations for each role: Customer, Seller, Rider, and Admin.

---

## 1. Customer Guide
- **Login/Registration:** Enter your phone number on the login page to receive an OTP (or use password `123456` in testing).
- **Browsing:** Use the homepage to search for products by category or brand.
- **Cart & Checkout:** Click "Add to Cart" on any product. Open the cart drawer, click Checkout, select your saved delivery address, and choose Cash on Delivery (COD).
- **Tracking:** View your Orders page to track the status (Pending -> Confirmed -> Assigned -> Out for Delivery -> Delivered).

## 2. Seller Guide
- **Dashboard:** View sales summaries, pending orders, and low-stock alerts.
- **Products:** Add new products from the catalog or request a missing product from the admin.
- **Inventory:** Update quantities and prices for products you sell.
- **Orders:** Mark confirmed orders as "Ready for Pickup" when packaged.

## 3. Rider Guide
- **Dashboard:** View newly assigned deliveries from the Admin.
- **Delivery Flow:**
  1. Accept Delivery.
  2. Mark as "Picked Up" once collected from the Seller.
  3. Mark as "Out for Delivery".
  4. Mark as "Delivered" and collect cash (COD).

## 4. Admin Guide
- **Dashboard:** Monitor system-wide orders, revenue, and active users.
- **User Management:** Approve Seller applications and register new Riders.
- **Catalog Management:** Add new baseline products to the universal catalog for Sellers to list.
- **Order Routing:** Assign available Riders to confirmed orders to trigger the delivery process.
